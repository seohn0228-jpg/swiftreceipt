SwiftReceipt

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

SwiftReceipt is a small payment receipt maker run by an independent developer based in South Korea. It is built so that your business data never leaves your device.

What stays on your device

Everything you type — your business details, client details, line items, logo, signature and saved receipts — is stored only in your own browser (local storage). Backup files you create are saved to your device and restored from your device; they are never uploaded to us. We do not run a server that receives or stores it, and we cannot see it. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, it is gone, and we cannot recover it.

Importing from SwiftBill

SwiftReceipt and SwiftBill are made by the same developer and run on the same website. When you choose “Import from SwiftBill”, SwiftReceipt reads the invoices SwiftBill saved in this same browser and copies the details you pick into your receipt. This happens entirely inside your browser; nothing is sent to us or anyone else, and SwiftBill’s data is not changed.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Gumroad. Gumroad collects your payment details and email address under its own privacy policy. We receive the order information Gumroad shares with sellers (such as your email address and license key) so that we can support your purchase.

License check

When you enter a license key, SwiftReceipt sends that key directly from your browser to Gumroad to confirm it is valid and to count the device. It checks the key again when you open SwiftReceipt (at most every 10 minutes) so refunded or disabled keys stop working. Only the license key and our product ID are sent, never receipt data.

No tracking

SwiftReceipt sets no cookies and uses no advertising or tracking scripts. If we add privacy-friendly visitor statistics later, this policy will say exactly what is counted, and it will never include anything you type into a receipt.

Email

If you email us, we use your message only to reply and keep it only as long as needed to help you.

Children

SwiftReceipt is a business tool and is not directed at children under 16.
