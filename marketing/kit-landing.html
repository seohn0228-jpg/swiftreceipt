Questions

How does one key unlock both apps?

Paste the key into either app’s “Unlock Pro”. The other app on the same browser picks it up automatically — even if it’s already open in another tab — and the browser only counts once toward your 3-browser limit.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once and keep Pro in both apps, plus any updates we choose to release.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your own browser. There is no account and no server that receives your invoices or receipts. Both apps include a backup file you keep on your device.

I already bought one app. Should I get the Kit?

The Kit is best if you don’t own either app yet. If you already have one, you can simply buy the other app on its own.

What if it’s not for me?

Email us within 7 days of purchase for a full refund. After a refund the key stops working in both apps.

Are the documents valid for taxes?

The apps format the details you enter into clear documents. What an invoice or receipt must contain depends on where you work, so check your local rules. They’re formatting tools, not tax advice.
