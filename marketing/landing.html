Questions

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once and keep Pro. Future updates we release are included.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your own browser. There is no account and no server that receives your receipts. Pro includes a backup file you keep on your device — clearing your browser data deletes what isn’t backed up.

Can I use it on my phone and laptop?

Yes. Your license key works on up to 3 browsers. Bought a new device or cleared your browser? Email us your Gumroad receipt and we’ll reset the count.

What if it’s not for me?

Email us within 7 days of purchase for a full refund — no long explanations needed.

Is a SwiftReceipt receipt valid for taxes?

SwiftReceipt formats the details you enter into a clear receipt. What a valid receipt must contain depends on where you work, so check your local rules. It’s a formatting tool, not tax advice.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in any modern browser — Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox — on desktop, tablet or phone.
