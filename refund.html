7-day refund

If Pro doesn't work for you, email swiftbill26@gmail.com within 7 days of purchase with your Gumroad receipt email, and we will refund the full amount you paid. No long explanations needed — a short note helps us improve.

How refunds work

  • Refunds are issued through Gumroad to your original payment method. Your bank may take a few business days to show it.
  • After a refund, the license key is deactivated. Pro turns off the next time SwiftReceipt checks the key online (when you open it). Your receipts stay in your browser.
  • After 7 days we generally can't offer refunds, except where the law requires it.

Try before you buy

The free version lets you build a full receipt and export up to 3 PDFs a month, so you can check the result before paying.
